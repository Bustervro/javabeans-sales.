# JavaBeans Coffee Sales Analysis

## Project Description
This project uses Python and Pandas to analyze coffee sales from 2021 and 2022. The goal is to understand sales trends and help JavaBeans make better business decisions.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Jupyter Notebook
- uv
- GitHub

## Part 1: DataFrame Creation
I created a Pandas DataFrame using coffee sales data from 2021 and 2022.

| Year | Americano | Latte | Espresso | Cappuccino |
|------|-----------|-------|----------|------------|
| 2021 | 5628 | 12426 | 852 | 8522 |
| 2022 | 6239 | 25384 | 756 | 7561 |

## Part 2: Total Sales
I used Pandas to calculate the total sales for each drink.

- Americano: 11,867
- Latte: 37,810
- Espresso: 1,608
- Cappuccino: 16,083

## Part 3: Year-over-Year Growth
I calculated the sales growth percentage from 2021 to 2022.

- Americano: +10.86%
- Latte: +104.28%
- Espresso: -11.27%
- Cappuccino: -11.28%

## Part 4: Best and Least-Selling Drinks

2021:
- Best-selling: Latte
- Least-selling: Espresso

2022:
- Best-selling: Latte
- Least-selling: Espresso

## Part 5: Business Insights and Strategy

Latte was the best-selling drink in both years. Its sales increased by 104.28%. JavaBeans should focus more on advertising Latte because customers are buying more of it. Americano also increased in sales and could be promoted more.

Espresso and Cappuccino sales decreased from 2021 to 2022. JavaBeans should try new flavors, discounts, or better marketing to improve sales before deciding to remove these drinks from the menu.

## Conclusion
This project helped me practice creating DataFrames, calculating totals, finding growth percentages, and understanding business sales using Pandas.
